WHAT THE HISTORY SAYS

Across ~100 chip and tech stocks since 2011: when a stock was in an uptrend, it was higher 3 months later 64% of the time (middle result +6%). In a downtrend: 62% (middle result +6%). For just the next week: 55% vs 54%. So the trend label tells you where a stock has been, not where it's going: in this group, up- and downtrends were followed by similar results. Use it to understand the situation, not as a buy or sell signal. These companies were mostly big winners, which makes dips look better in hindsight than they will always be.
What do these words mean?
Uptrend / downtrendWhether the stock's average price over the last ~2½ months is above or below its average over the last ~10 months. It changes slowly, every few months.
Dipping / bouncingA short-term move against the bigger trend. Dips inside an uptrend are common and often don't end it.
Normal weekHow much this stock usually moves in a week. Bigger than that is unusual; twice that is rare.
How wildCalm (under ±4% a week), Normal (±4–7%), Wild (±7–11%), Very wild (more).
EarningsThe company's quarterly results. The stock often jumps or drops a lot the next day.
